## 1. Honest audit

### What works
| Good thing | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Colours are consistent (dark blue) | The grid looks like one brand. Keep the colours. |
| Logo is clear | People can remember the name. |
| Some ideas are good ("Before vs after a website", "AI illusion vs real engineering") | These topics are useful. They are just told in the wrong way (as ads). |
| You post at all | Many studios never start. You have the habit. |

### What does not work, and why
| Problem | Why it hurts |
|---|---|
| **Every post is a sales ad** | People follow to learn or to be entertained. Ads give them nothing, so they don't follow, save, or share. Instagram then shows you to fewer people. |
| **No face, no human** | People buy from people. A small studio's biggest advantage is "you talk directly to the founder". Right now nobody knows who you are. |
| **No real work shown** | You have 6 real paid projects. A buyer sees only posters. They think "maybe they have never built anything". |
| **AI-made images look generic** | Business owners have seen thousands of these. They look like every other agency. Real screenshots feel more trustworthy. |
| **Too much text on the posters** | On a phone, dense text is hard to read. People scroll past in less than 1 second. |
| **Logo animation reels** | Nobody watches a logo. No hook, no value, so reach is very low. |
| **No process, no prices, no story** | Buyers want to know: How does it work? How much? Who are you? The account answers none of these. |
| **No clear next step** | No "DM me X" or "link in bio for free audit". Interested people don't know what to do. |

**What to do with old posts:** Archive the weakest ones (do not delete — you can bring them back). Keep 3–5 best. Pin 3 new posts at the top once you have them: (1) your story reel, (2) a real project reel, (3) the prices carousel.

## 2. New positioning (who you are, in one line)

> **"A small, founder-led studio that builds websites, online stores and AI automation for growing businesses — fixed price, you own the code, and you talk directly to the person who builds it."**

Why this works:
- **Founder-led** = you speak to the builder, not a salesperson. Big agencies cannot say this.
- **Fixed price** = no surprise bills. Clients love this.
- **You own the code** = no lock-in. Many cheap agencies keep the code. This builds trust.
- **India + worldwide** = good quality at a fair price for foreign clients.

Say these 3 things again and again: **fixed price, you own the code, talk to the founder.**

Simple rule: **for every 1 sales post, make 9 useful or real posts.**

## 6. Visual rules

### Carousels
| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Size | **1080 × 1350 px** (portrait 4:5) |
| Slides | **6–8 slides** |
| Text size | Big. Headline at least 60–80 px. Body at least 36–40 px. If you can't read it on your phone at arm's length, it is too small. |
| One idea per slide | Max 20–25 words per slide |
| Slide 1 | The hook. A clear promise or a problem. ("Your website loses customers in 5 seconds. Here's why.") |
| Last slide | One call to action: "Save this", "DM AUDIT", or "Follow for more" — only one |
| Brand | Keep dark blue background, white text, one accent colour (e.g. yellow or cyan) for important words |
| Real screenshots | Use real screenshots of client sites (with permission) instead of AI images |
| Safe area | Keep text away from the bottom 150 px and the edges — the grid now crops some posts |
| Tool | Canva or Figma. Make one template and reuse it every time. |

### Reels
| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Size | **9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920 px** |
| Length | **15–40 seconds** |
| Hook | **First 1.5 seconds** must show the topic. Text on screen + you saying it. No logo intro. |
| Captions | **Burned in** (on the video). Most people watch without sound. Use CapCut or Instagram's auto-captions. |
| Safe area | Keep text in the middle. The top 250 px and bottom 400 px are covered by Instagram buttons. |
| Sound | Clear voice > music. Use a cheap clip-on mic if possible. Music low in background. |
| Face | Show your face in at least 1 of every 3 reels |
| Screen recordings | Record your phone or laptop screen. Zoom in on the important part. |
| Light | Face a window. Do not sit with a window behind you. |
| Cover | Make a cover with 3–5 words so the grid looks clean |

Start with these. After 4 weeks, check **Insights → Total followers → Most active times** and adjust.

|---|---|---|
| **India** | 12:30–1:30 pm, 7:30–9:30 pm | same |

| **UAE** (GST) | 12–2 pm, 7–9 pm | 1:30–3:30 pm, 8:30–10:30 pm IST |
| **US East** (winter, EST) | 7–9 am, 12–1 pm | 5:30–7:30 pm, 10:30–11:30 pm IST |
| **Australia East** (summer, AEDT) | 7–9 am, 7–9 pm | 1:30–3:30 am, 1:30–3:30 pm IST |

**Simple plan:** post at **7:30 pm IST** most days (good for India, and early morning US East). On 2 days a week, post at **1:30 pm IST** (good for UK/UAE lunch, Australia evening).

Note: clocks change. UK moved to GMT on 25 Oct 2026. US moves to winter time on 1 Nov 2026. Check a time converter if unsure.

## 8. Hashtags — use 3 to 5, not 30

Instagram now works more like a search engine. 30 hashtags look like spam. Use 3–5 that describe the exact post.

Formula: **1 topic + 1 niche + 1 location/audience + 1–2 specific**

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Topic | #webdesign #ecommercewebsite #aiautomation #whatsappbot |
| Niche (who it helps) | #d2cbrand #smallbusinesstips #shopifyalternative #manufacturingindia |
| Location / audience | #indianbusiness #dubaibusiness #ukSmallBusiness #{yourcity}business |
| Specific | #mernstack #websitespeed #chatbot #calisthenicsequipment (only when the post is about it) |

Do not use huge tags like #love #business #instagood. You will be lost in millions of posts.

## 9. Captions for Instagram search (keywords)

People search Instagram like Google now: "website design India", "WhatsApp chatbot for business".

Rules:
- **First line = hook + keyword.** Example: "Website design for small businesses: 5 mistakes that lose customers."
- Use the words your client would type: *website, online store, e-commerce, WhatsApp bot, booking, clinic, restaurant, Dubai, UK*.
- Put the keyword in: the caption, the on-screen text, and say it out loud in reels (Instagram reads audio too).
- Write 3–8 short lines. Short sentences. Line breaks.
- End with **one** clear action: "Save this for later" or "Comment SITE and I'll send you a free check".
- Add **Alt text** on carousels (Advanced settings → Accessibility → Write alt text) with a short keyword sentence.

## 10. Daily engagement routine (15 minutes)

Do this right after you post, and once more in the day if you can.

| Minutes | Action |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Reply to every comment and DM on your account. |
| 5–10 | Leave **5 real comments** on posts by **target clients** (D2C brands, equipment makers, clinics, cafés). Say something specific, e.g. "Love the new packaging — the product photos on your site would look great with this too." No selling in comments. |
| 10–15 | Leave **5 comments** on posts by **peers** (other developers, designers, founders). They grow with you and can send referrals. |

Rules:
- Comments must be 1–2 real sentences. Not "Nice 🔥".
- Never paste your link in someone's comments.
- Follow 5 new target accounts per day. Unfollow nobody in bulk.
- Watch stories of your target clients and reply to one story a day with a genuine reaction. This starts conversations naturally.

## 11. How DMs turn into leads (comment-keyword flow)

```
Post says: "Comment AUDIT and I'll check your website for free"
        ↓
Person comments "AUDIT"
        ↓
You (or an auto-DM tool) send a DM within 1 hour
        ↓
DM asks 2 questions: website link + what they sell
        ↓
You record a 2-minute screen video (Loom) reviewing their site
        ↓
Send video + "Want me to fix these 3 things? Here's what it would cost."
        ↓
Move them to WhatsApp or a call → proposal → 40% advance
```

**First DM (copy this):**
> Hi {name}, thanks for commenting! I'm Mohamed from GOFS Tech.
> Send me your website link and tell me what you sell — I'll record a short free video showing 3 things to improve. No cost, no pressure.

**After you send the video:**
> Here's your video: {link}. The 3 big fixes are {1}, {2}, {3}.
> If you want, I can do this for you. A simple fix like this usually fits our {package} plan, from {price}. Happy to answer any questions here or on WhatsApp.

**Automation:** later you can use a Meta-approved tool like ManyChat to send the first DM automatically when someone comments a keyword. Start manually first — personal replies convert better while you are small.

Keywords to use: **AUDIT** (website check), **BOT** (AI chatbot demo), **PRICE** (price list), **STORE** (e-commerce info).

## 12. What to measure every week (not just likes)

Every Sunday, open Insights and fill this table in a Google Sheet.

| Metric | What it tells you | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| **Saves** | People found it useful. Strongest sign for Teach posts. | Post → View insights |
| **Shares** (sends) | People showed it to someone. This brings new followers. | Post → View insights |
| **Profile visits** | People wanted to know who you are | Insights → Accounts reached |
| **Link taps / website taps** | People wanted to buy or learn more | Insights |
| **DMs started** | Real conversations — closest to money | Count in your inbox |
| **Keyword comments** (AUDIT, BOT) | Direct leads | Count manually |
| **Reel 3-second hold / average watch time** | Is the hook working? | Reel → View insights |
| **Non-follower reach %** | Are new people seeing you? | Post insights |
| **Followers gained** | Slow signal. Check, but don't chase it. | Insights |

**Which posts to repeat:** each week find the top 2 posts by **saves + shares**. Make a new version of those topics next week.
**Which hooks to fix:** if a reel's average watch time is under 3 seconds, the first 1.5 seconds failed. Change the hook, not the topic.
