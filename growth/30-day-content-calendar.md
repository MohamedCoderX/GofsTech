### Day 2 — Wed 28 Oct — CAROUSEL — Teach

**Title:** 5 things that make customers leave your website in 5 seconds
**8 slides, 1080 × 1350**

**Slide 1 (hook)**
> **Customers leave your website in 5 seconds.**
> Here are 5 reasons why. (And how to fix each one.)

**Slide 2**
> **1. It loads too slowly**
> Many visitors leave if a page takes more than about 3 seconds on a phone.
> **Fix:** compress images, use good hosting, remove heavy plugins.

**Slide 3**
> **2. It's hard to use on a phone**
> Most visitors come from mobile. Tiny text and buttons = they leave.
> **Fix:** test your site on your own phone today. Can you tap every button with your thumb?

**Slide 4**
> **3. They can't tell what you do**
> If the first screen says only "Welcome", visitors are confused.
> **Fix:** one clear line — *what you sell + who it's for + where.*

**Slide 5**
> **4. No easy way to contact you**
> No WhatsApp button. No phone number at the top. Contact form hidden at the bottom.
> **Fix:** add a WhatsApp/call button that is always visible.

**Slide 6**
> **5. It looks old or untrusted**
> Old design, broken links, no real photos, no prices, no reviews.
> **Fix:** real photos, a clean layout, and a reason to trust you (work, reviews, years in business).

**Slide 7 (checklist)**
> **Quick check — your website:**
> ☐ Loads in under 3 seconds
> ☐ Works well on a phone
> ☐ Says clearly what you do
> ☐ Has a WhatsApp / call button
> ☐ Looks current and trustworthy

## Part 3 — After day 7: how to write the rest

For days 8–30, use the outline in the table and this simple recipe:

**Carousel recipe (6–8 slides)**
1. Hook: a problem or a promise, max 10 words
2. Why it matters (1 slide)
3–6. One tip / step per slide, each with a "Fix:" line
7. Short checklist or summary
8. One action: Save / Comment keyword / DM

**Reel recipe (15–40s)**
1. 0–1.5s: say the result or the problem, with text on screen
2. Show the real screen / real work
3. 3 short steps or points
4. End with your face + one keyword ("AUDIT", "BOT", "STORE", "PRICE")

**Real-work reels checklist:** client said yes · no private data on screen · say the real website name · link in stories · add to "Work" highlight.

**Batch work:** film 3–4 reels on Sunday morning, design 3–4 carousels on Wednesday evening. Then daily posting takes 15 minutes.
