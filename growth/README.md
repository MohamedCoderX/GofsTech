## The 3 biggest problems

1. **Nobody can see your real work.**
   You have built real sites (FITZ1, Reverse Rituals, Aimaire Inc, Nancy Equipments, Thzed, Ashley Jude).
   But Instagram shows only AI-made posters. A buyer cannot see proof. Proof is what makes people pay.

2. **Every post is an ad.**
   People do not follow ads. They follow accounts that teach them something or show something real.
   If every post says "buy from us", people scroll past and Instagram shows your posts to fewer people.

3. **You wait for clients to come. They will not come by themselves.**
   62 followers cannot bring enough work. Instagram is slow. You need to go and talk to the right businesses every day (outreach), and use Instagram as your "proof shop window" when they check you.

---

## The plan in 5 points

- **Pick 2 niches** (types of clients) and talk to them every day. Recommended: D2C e-commerce brands, and B2B equipment / manufacturing companies. You already have real projects in both.
- **Show your face and your real work.** Post screen recordings of real sites, your process, your story. Teach business owners useful things. Only 1 post in 10 is a sales post.
- **Send 20 personal messages every weekday.** Use the "free 2-minute video audit" idea. Follow the legal rules for cold email.
- **Get proof.** Ask your 6 past clients for a real testimonial this week. Write one case study page per project on the new website.
- **Sell monthly plans, not only one-time projects.** Instagram management, video retainers, care plans and AI automation fees bring money every month. This is how small studios become stable companies.

> **Update (Oct 2026):** prices are no longer public. The website shows the price guide only after someone submits the form. GOFS Tech does not sell SEO or paid ads; it now sells video production, Instagram management and branding as well as software and AI.

## One honest note

Growth will feel slow for the first 4–6 weeks. That is normal.
Followers are not money. **Conversations are money.**
Track: messages sent, replies, calls, proposals, signed clients. If those go up, you are winning — even if followers stay small.
